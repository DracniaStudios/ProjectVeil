[ Added ]

- Task stations now emit a periodic SOUND_STATION noise (0.6, every 0.9s) while
  their minigame runs. It is emitted at the station, never at the player, so
  perception is never handed a live position under cover of a world fact.
- Interacting with a task station emits a one-shot SOUND_TAMPER noise (0.8) at
  the station. A locked station that refuses to open still makes the noise —
  a silent failed attempt would otherwise be the safest way to play.
- Physics collisions emit SOUND_IMPACT scaled by closing speed along the contact
  normal (silent below 2 u/s, full loudness at 12). Hung off the first-contact
  branch so one bang is one noise, not one per solver iteration, and attributed
  to the faster body so a stalker brushing a wall does not chase itself.
- tests/EmitterTests.cpp — 28 checks over the three emitters above: position,
  loudness, attribution, one-shot/periodic cadence, and the resting-contact case
  that decides whether walking a room floods the sound field.

[ Updated ]

- tests/run_tests.sh builds and runs the engine-linked suites from one loop, so
  adding a suite is a line rather than another copy of the g++ invocation. A
  failing suite no longer hides the state of the next.

[ Fixed ]

- InteractableObject::isRunningMiniGame was a one-way latch: ActivateMiniGame set
  it and nothing ever cleared it, so a station counted as occupied for the rest
  of the session. The Director skips occupied stations, so every station the
  player ever touched was permanently excluded from hinting. ReleaseMiniGame now
  clears it, and ResetMiniGame restores it across a replay so a retried station
  does not fall silent.
